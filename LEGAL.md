The "Target Recon" scanner feature of the Platform operates **strictly via passive, non-invasive Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)**:
* Queries are limited to public recursive DNS resolution (via DNS-over-HTTPS) and standard public cryptographic validation (SPF, DMARC, MX records).
