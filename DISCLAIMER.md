# Threat Intelligence Disclaimer

**Titan Cyber Command** provides real-time situational awareness of publicly reported cyber incidents, exploited vulnerabilities, and infrastructure anomalies.

---

### Key Principles

1. **Honesty Over Sensation**:
   * Claims made by extortionists on dark-web leak sites are classified as **"Claimed"** until verified. Threat actors frequently fabricate claims or exaggerate incident impact for reputational leverage.
   * Press reports are classified as **"Reported"** until confirmed by the affected organization, an official regulatory filing (e.g. SEC Form 8-K), or a governing CERT body.

2. **Absence of Evidence is Not Evidence of Safety**:
   * The absence of your organization, infrastructure, or technology stack from this map does not guarantee safety or immunity from compromise. Zero-day exploits and undisclosed intrusions exist outside public reporting visibility.

3. **Passive Research Only**:
   * Reconnaissance features (such as Target Recon) use passive public DNS records only. No active penetration testing or unauthorized port scanning is performed against any host.

4. **Hypothetical Simulation**:
   * WarGames simulations represent theoretical catastrophe models for defensive resilience exercises only. No actual infrastructure is manipulated.

5. **Not Legal or Incident Response Advice**:
   * Intelligence reports, CVE dossiers, and automated remediation suggestions provided by the Platform are for educational and advisory reference. Organizations undergoing an active cyber security incident should immediately engage certified digital forensics and incident response (DFIR) specialists and legal counsel.
